Run a Dash identity as a group

A multisig identity belongs to a group instead of one person. The members make its keys together, and it only acts when enough of them sign: two of three, three of five, whatever the group chooses. Nobody ever holds a whole key, not even this site.

How it works

  1. Start a group and send the invite link to the other members.
  2. Check fingerprints with each other on a call, so you know who joined.
  3. Make the keys together. Each page creates its member's share; nobody sees the others.
  4. Fund the identity. One member locks DASH from their own address, and the group signs its creation.
  5. Propose and sign. Payments, documents and key changes go out once enough members sign.
